RETURN &
SHIPPING POLICY

ALL SALES ARE FINAL

All Arivon Apparel products are made to order. Because each item is printed or sewn specifically for you, we do not accept returns or exchanges for reasons such as sizing, change of mind, or personal preference. Please refer to the size guide on each product page before placing your order.

WHEN WE WILL MAKE IT RIGHT

We will issue a replacement or full refund only in the following cases:

  • Your item arrived damaged (torn, broken, physically defective)
  • You received the wrong item (wrong product, size, or design)

To be eligible, you must contact us within 7 days of receiving your order. Claims submitted after 7 days will not be accepted.

HOW TO FILE A CLAIM

Email us at arivonapparel@gmail.com with the subject line "ORDER ISSUE" and include the following:

  • Your order number
  • A clear photo of the item showing the issue
  • A brief description of the problem

We'll respond within 2 business days and make it right.

SHIPPING

All Arivon Apparel items are made to order. Production typically takes 3–7 business days before your order ships. Shipping times vary by location but are generally 7–14 business days total for domestic orders within the United States.

Once your order ships you will receive a tracking number via email. Arivon Apparel is not responsible for delays caused by the carrier or customs.

SIZING

We are not able to process returns or exchanges due to incorrect sizing. Each product page includes a detailed size guide — please review it carefully before purchasing. If you have any sizing questions before ordering, contact us and we'll help.

Questions? Reach us at arivonapparel@gmail.com
