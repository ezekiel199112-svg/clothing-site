WE ARE
ARIVON.

Arivon Apparel started with a simple pull: a love for fashion, and the need to build something that was actually mine.

We're not a big team with a boardroom and a five-year plan. It's one person figuring things out, one drop at a time — designing pieces rooted in street culture for people who are doing the same thing we are: still figuring out who they are, and doing it in their own clothes instead of borrowed ones.

We don't have it all figured out yet. That's kind of the point.

OUR MISSION

To make streetwear for people figuring it out as they go — built with the same honesty we're building the brand with.
