WE ARE
ARIVON.

Arivon Apparel was born from a simple idea: streetwear should mean something.

Founded at 14 years old out of a passion for fashion and a hunger to build something real, Arivon started as a vision and grew into a brand. Based in the United States, we design clothing rooted in street culture — pieces that hit different because they come from an authentic place, not a boardroom.

We're not just selling hoodies. We're building a movement for people who create before they're ready, who start before they have permission. That's the Arivon way.

OUR MISSION

To make streetwear that represents the grind — built by the youth, worn by the bold, and designed to last beyond the trend.
